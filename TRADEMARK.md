# Hachi trademark policy

This is a plain-language policy, not legal advice. It may be revised before
the marks are registered.

The source code in this repository is licensed under Apache-2.0 (see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)). Apache-2.0 section 6 does not grant any right to use the
project's names or artwork. This policy says what you may do with them.

## What is covered

- The names **Hachi** and **小八**, as the name of a software product. (ハチ is
  only the Japanese reading and is not separately claimed.)
- The Hachi dog logo and character, as a brand: the artwork in
  `docs/assets/`, `docs/hachi.png` and the app bundle.

The copyright in these files is covered by Apache-2.0 like the rest of the
repository, so you may copy and modify them as the license says, and a fork may keep
the image files in its source tree. What this policy limits is using the
name or logo as the **identity** of your own product: its name, app icon,
store listing, website header or packaging.

## What you may do without asking

- Fork, modify and redistribute the code under Apache-2.0.
- Say truthfully that your work is "based on Hachi" or "a fork of Hachi",
  and link to this repository.
- Use the name in plain text to refer to the upstream project, in articles,
  reviews, issues and documentation.

## What needs written permission

- Releasing a fork or derived product under the name Hachi / 小八, or under a
  name or logo that is confusingly similar.
- Using the logo or character artwork as the identity of your own product
  (app icon, header, packaging).
- Suggesting that your product is official, endorsed by or affiliated with
  the Hachi maintainers.
- Using the name or logo as the name or identity of your own package,
  domain, service or app-store listing (a description that says "a fork of
  Hachi" is fine).

A fork that changes the name and artwork needs no permission.

## Modified builds

Private or internal use of a modified copy needs no permission. If you
publicly distribute a modified version, give it your own name and logo.
